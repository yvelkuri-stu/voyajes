# Age / content rating notes

Voyajes has no violence, sex, drugs, gambling or profanity of its own. The rating is driven by **user-generated content**
(guests can post photos and comments) and by **sharing links**.

## Apple — age rating questionnaire
- All "Cartoon/Fantasy/Realistic Violence, Sexual Content, Nudity, Profanity, Horror, Medical, Alcohol/Drugs, Gambling, Contests": **None**
- **User-generated content: Yes** · **Messaging and chat: No** (comments only, no DMs) · **Unrestricted web access: No**
- Expect **13+** (Apple's 2025 scale; "12+" on the old scale). Don't choose a kids' category.

## Google Play — IARC questionnaire
- Category: **Utility, productivity, communication or other**
- Violence / fear / sexuality / language / controlled substances / gambling: **No** to everything
- "Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content?" **Yes** (comments, photos) → **"Users interact"** descriptor
- "Shares user location with other users?" **No** (only an approximate route map the host chooses to publish)
- "Allows purchase of digital goods?" **No**
- Expected result: **Everyone / PEGI 3 with "Users Interact"** (some regions give Teen/12 because of UGC — that's fine).
- Target audience (Play "Target audience and content"): **13+** (don't include under-13 — that triggers the Families policy).

## Microsoft Store
Same IARC questionnaire as Play; answer identically.
